Our mission

Security has always meant protecting the future. We believe that now includes the planet it happens on.

Founded on the principle that resilience and responsibility are the same thing, Meridian designs aircraft, satellites and intelligence systems that run on solar, hydrogen and synthetic fuel — and pairs them with the climate analytics governments need to decarbonise entire economies by 2040.

High-altitude pseudo-satellite

Halcyon

A solar-electric aircraft that flies for months at 65,000 ft. Surveillance, disaster response and atmospheric monitoring — with zero fuel and zero emissions.

  • 180-day endurance

Sustainable living, built in

The first carbon-negative aerospace campus.

Our Meridian Works campus is built from mass timber, cooled by living walls and powered entirely by on-site solar. Engineers live within walking distance in our net-positive housing community — because how we build is as important as what we build.

How we work

Three commitments. No exceptions.

01

Clean propulsion

Every platform we deliver after 2028 runs on solar, hydrogen or certified synthetic fuel.

02

Climate intelligence

Orbital and aerial data turned into decarbonisation roadmaps for ministries and boardrooms.

03

Circular manufacturing

94% of every airframe is recovered and reused. Nothing we make is designed to become waste.
